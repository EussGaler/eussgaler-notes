# <span style="color:#c62828"><strong>常用变换公式汇总</strong></span>

## <span style="color:#c62828"><strong>1. 傅里叶级数（CFS）</strong></span>

### <span style="color:#c62828"><strong>（1）三角形式</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
\begin{aligned}
f(t)
&=a_0+\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty}
\left[a_n\cos(n\omega t)+b_n\sin(n\omega t)\right]\\
&=c_0+\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty}
c_n\cos(n\omega t+\varphi_n).
\end{aligned}}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828"><strong>其中</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
c_0=a_0=\frac1T\int_{-T/2}^{T/2}f(t)\,\mathrm dt.}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
a_n=\frac2T\int_{-T/2}^{T/2}f(t)\cos(n\omega t)\,\mathrm dt,\qquad
b_n=\frac2T\int_{-T/2}^{T/2}f(t)\sin(n\omega t)\,\mathrm dt.}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
c_n=\sqrt{a_n^2+b_n^2},\qquad
\varphi_n=-\arctan\frac{b_n}{a_n}.}
$$

### <span style="color:#c62828"><strong>（2）复指数形式</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
f(t)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}F_ne^{jn\omega t}.}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828"><strong>其中</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
F_n=\frac{a_n-jb_n}{2}
=\frac12c_ne^{j\varphi_n}.}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828"><strong>$F_n$ 与 $F_{-n}$ 互为共轭。</strong></span>

## <span style="color:#c62828"><strong>2. 非周期信号的傅里叶变换（CTFT）</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}f(t)\xleftrightarrow{\mathcal F} F(\omega).}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
F(\omega)=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f(t)e^{-j\omega t}\,\mathrm dt,}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
f(t)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}
F(\omega)e^{j\omega t}\,\mathrm d\omega.}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828"><strong>性质：P8。</strong></span>

<span style="color:#c62828"><strong>周期信号的傅里叶变换：</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
\mathcal F[e^{j\omega_0t}]=2\pi\delta(\omega-\omega_0).}
$$

## <span style="color:#c62828"><strong>3. 拉普拉斯变换</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}f(t)\xleftrightarrow{\mathcal L}F(s).}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
F(s)=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f(t)e^{-st}\,\mathrm dt,}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
f(t)=\frac1{2\pi j}
\int_{\sigma-j\infty}^{\sigma+j\infty}
F(s)e^{st}\,\mathrm ds.}
$$

## <span style="color:#c62828"><strong>4. 离散傅里叶级数（DFS）</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}x(n)\xleftrightarrow{\mathrm{DFS}}X(k\Omega_0).}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828"><strong>对一个周期 $T$ 进行 $N$ 点采样，则</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
T=NT_s,\qquad
\omega_0=\frac{2\pi}{T}=\frac{2\pi}{NT_s},
\qquad \Omega_0=\frac{2\pi}{N}=\omega_0T_s.}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
X_k=X(k\Omega_0)=\frac1N\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}x(n)e^{-jk\Omega_0n},
\qquad k=0,1,\ldots,N-1,}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
x(n)=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}X(k\Omega_0)e^{jk\Omega_0n},
\qquad n=0,1,\ldots,N-1.}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828"><strong>性质：P14。</strong></span>

## <span style="color:#c62828"><strong>5. 离散时间傅里叶变换（DTFT）</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}x(n)\xleftrightarrow{\mathrm{DTFT}}X(\Omega).}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828"><strong>（或 $X(e^{j\Omega})$）</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
X(\Omega)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}x(n)e^{-j\Omega n},}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
x(n)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}
X(\Omega)e^{j\Omega n}\,\mathrm d\Omega.}
$$

## <span style="color:#c62828"><strong>6. 离散傅里叶变换（DFT）</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}x(n)\xleftrightarrow{\mathrm{DFT}}X(k).}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
X(k)=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}x(n)e^{-jk\frac{2\pi}{N}n},
\qquad k=0,1,\ldots,N-1,}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
x(n)=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}X(k)e^{jk\frac{2\pi}{N}n},
\qquad n=0,1,\ldots,N-1.}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828"><strong>性质：P15。</strong></span>

## <span style="color:#c62828"><strong>7. $z$ 变换</strong></span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
X(z)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}x(n)z^{-n},}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
x(n)=\frac1{2\pi j}\oint_C X(z)z^{n-1}\,\mathrm dz.}
$$
