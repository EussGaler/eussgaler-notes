# <span style="color:#c62828">常用变换公式汇总</span>

## <span style="color:#c62828">1. 傅里叶级数（CFS）</span>

### <span style="color:#c62828">（1）三角形式</span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
\begin{aligned}
f(t)
&=a_0+\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty}
\left[a_n\cos(n\omega_0t)+b_n\sin(n\omega_0t)\right]\\
&=c_0+\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty}
c_n\cos(n\omega_0t+\varphi_n),\qquad \omega_0=\frac{2\pi}{T}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
c_0=a_0=\frac1T\int_{-T/2}^{T/2}f(t)\,\mathrm dt.}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
a_n=\frac2T\int_{-T/2}^{T/2}f(t)\cos(n\omega_0t)\,\mathrm dt,\qquad
b_n=\frac2T\int_{-T/2}^{T/2}f(t)\sin(n\omega_0t)\,\mathrm dt.}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
c_n=\sqrt{a_n^2+b_n^2},\qquad
\varphi_n=\operatorname{atan2}(-b_n,a_n).}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828">相位需根据象限确定；$c_n=0$ 时该项相位无影响。</span>

### <span style="color:#c62828">（2）复指数形式</span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
f(t)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}F_ne^{jn\omega_0t},
\qquad
F_n=\frac1T\int_{-T/2}^{T/2}f(t)e^{-jn\omega_0t}\,\mathrm dt.}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
F_0=a_0,\qquad
F_n=\frac{a_n-jb_n}{2}
=\frac{c_n}{2}e^{j\varphi_n}\quad(n>0).}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828">对于实信号，$F_{-n}=F_n^*$。</span>

## <span style="color:#c62828">2. 连续时间傅里叶变换（CTFT）</span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}f(t)\longleftrightarrow F(\omega).}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
F(\omega)=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f(t)e^{-j\omega t}\,\mathrm dt,}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
f(t)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}
F(\omega)e^{j\omega t}\,\mathrm d\omega.}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828">性质见</span>[连续信号的分析](02-连续信号的分析.md)<span style="color:#c62828">中“傅里叶变换”的有关内容。</span>

<span style="color:#c62828">周期信号的傅里叶变换可用冲激表示，例如：</span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
e^{j\omega_0t}\longleftrightarrow2\pi\delta(\omega-\omega_0).}
$$

## <span style="color:#c62828">3. 拉普拉斯变换</span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
F(s)=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f(t)e^{-st}\,\mathrm dt,}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
f(t)=\frac1{2\pi j}
\int_{\sigma-j\infty}^{\sigma+j\infty}
F(s)e^{st}\,\mathrm ds.}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828">积分直线取在收敛域内。</span>

## <span style="color:#c62828">4. 离散傅里叶级数（DFS）</span>

<span style="color:#c62828">周期为 $T$，每周期取 $N$ 个样值，取样间隔为 $T_s$：</span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
T=NT_s,\qquad
\omega_0=\frac{2\pi}{T}=\frac{2\pi}{NT_s},
\qquad \Omega_0=\frac{2\pi}{N}=\omega_0T_s.}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
X(k)=\frac1N\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}x(n)e^{-jk\Omega_0n},}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
x(n)=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}X(k)e^{jk\Omega_0n}.}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828">性质见</span>[离散信号的分析](03-离散信号的分析.md)<span style="color:#c62828">中的“离散傅里叶级数”。</span>

## <span style="color:#c62828">5. 离散时间傅里叶变换（DTFT）</span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}x(n)\longleftrightarrow X(\Omega)=X(e^{j\Omega}).}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
X(\Omega)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}x(n)e^{-j\Omega n},}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
x(n)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}
X(\Omega)e^{j\Omega n}\,\mathrm d\Omega.}
$$

## <span style="color:#c62828">6. 离散傅里叶变换（DFT）</span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}x(n)\longleftrightarrow X(k).}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
X(k)=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}x(n)e^{-j\frac{2\pi}{N}kn},
\qquad k=0,\ldots,N-1,}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
x(n)=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}X(k)e^{j\frac{2\pi}{N}kn},
\qquad n=0,\ldots,N-1.}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828">性质见</span>[离散信号的分析](03-离散信号的分析.md)<span style="color:#c62828">中的“离散傅里叶变换”。</span>

## <span style="color:#c62828">7. $z$ 变换</span>

$$
{\color{#c62828}
X(z)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{+\infty}x(n)z^{-n},}
$$

$$
{\color{#c62828}
x(n)=\frac1{2\pi j}\oint_C X(z)z^{n-1}\,\mathrm dz.}
$$

<span style="color:#c62828">$C$ 是位于收敛域内、逆时针绕原点一周的闭合围线。</span>
